Downloaded academic papers often arrive with filenames like `2401.12345.pdf`, `document.pdf`, or `main.pdf`.

PaperRenamer is the Day 001 project: a small desktop tool that extracts a likely title, author, and year from a PDF, then generates a cleaner filename such as `2026_Smith_Vision_Transformer_for_Medical_Imaging.pdf`.
