Deskling is a small companion for people who build things alone at a desk.

The first version keeps the idea intentionally simple: a tiny character sits near the edge of the screen, wakes when you are active, gets sleepy when you drift away, and celebrates when you press an "I shipped something" button.

Later versions can react to commits, local build results, or GitHub activity. For Day 001, the important thing is making a small working object with a little warmth.
