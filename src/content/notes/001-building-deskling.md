## Why I built it

Working alone can make building feel quiet in a way that is both peaceful and a little too silent. Deskling starts from a simple question: what if the desktop had a tiny companion that noticed the rhythm of making?

## What problem it solves

This is not a serious productivity system. It is a small emotional tool for people who spend long hours writing, coding, studying, or shipping small projects. It gives the workspace a visible pulse.

## How it should work

The first version should be tiny. It should show a character with a few states: active, idle, sleepy, and celebrating. A manual "I shipped something" action is enough for v0.1. Commit detection and build reactions can wait.

## What I want to learn

The interesting part is whether a small desktop object can feel motivating without becoming distracting. The goal is not gamification. The goal is a little presence.
